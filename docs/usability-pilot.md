# Usability validation

Status: protocol prepared; participant sessions have not been conducted.

Run moderated sessions in an isolated test workspace with synthetic records.
Include business owners, accounting-firm staff and an administrator, plus buyer
reviewers before enabling that workflow. Include keyboard and screen-reader
users and participants on narrow mobile screens. Obtain consent before recording;
do not record passwords, authentication codes or real customer data.

## Tasks

1. Explain who Valo is for, request access, then find sign-in as a returning user.
2. Find password recovery without requesting a reset for another person's account.
3. Find today's most important work and identify completed and outstanding setup steps.
4. Create an unfinished invoice with a missing price, explain the checklist, then correct it.
5. Leave two unnumbered drafts and find the right one using its description, total and save time.
6. Explain whether a draft is saved on the device or account, its expiry, and how to resume on another device.
7. Disconnect the test network during creation, reconnect and recover the original result without creating a replacement.
8. Edit the same invoice in two test sessions, compare the conflict and explain what each recovery action does.
9. Find a secondary tool using navigation and search; return to the invoice without losing context.
10. Query a buyer confirmation, explain that response's permanence, then respond to a new supplier request.

For task 10, use the existing supplier re-request workflow on a stamped invoice.
Do not edit or delete the earlier confirmation history. Do not send real payments,
authority submissions, invitations or emails during these sessions.

## Evidence

For each task record a pseudonymous participant ID, role, viewport/input method,
completion without assistance, completion time, errors, recovery success,
confidence (1 to 5), and a short observed quote or behavior. Mark an assisted
completion as assisted, not successful unaided completion. Record network and
screen-reader conditions explicitly. Keep session evidence outside the repository.

Compare the same tasks before and after changes. Prioritize inability to finish,
misunderstood save/submission states, accidental duplicate work, and keyboard or
screen-reader blockers before preferences about appearance. Do not aggregate
small-sample observations into claims of accessibility certification.

## Release decision

No unresolved task blocker or mistaken irreversible action should be accepted
for an enabled launch workflow. Record owners and follow-up dates for lower-risk
findings. Update the existing release-readiness usability evidence only after
real sessions have been completed and reviewed; automated fixtures are separate
engineering evidence, not substitutes for participant results.
